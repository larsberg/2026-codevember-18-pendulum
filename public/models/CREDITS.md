For this sketch the assets were staged in Blender, textures were resized and re-encoded, and the meshes were re-exported with meshopt compression. The clock and vase received a clearcoat finish. The white metronome, stage, lighting rigs, floating television and its test card, turntable, speakers, books, dishes and table lamp were modeled for this sketch.

The lacquered counter, plant stands and foliage, washers and laundry basket, the Eclipse pinball machine and its playfield and backglass artwork, the woven rugs, and the tabletop orrery were also modeled in Blender for this sketch. The Citrus soda fountain, stools, fruit and lever press, and the Nocturne projector, trolley and cinema seat are original models.
